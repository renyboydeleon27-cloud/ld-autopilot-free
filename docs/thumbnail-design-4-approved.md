## Approved geometry

Reference ratio: **864 × 1536 (9:16)**. Canonical render canvas: **1080 × 1920**.

- Compose all essential poster content at roughly **82–85% visual scale**, centered on event-specific background bleed.
- Keep essential content inside about **x 8–92% / y 10–90%**.
- Title block: **y 11–30%**.
- Main headline: **y 11–21%**.
- Yellow strip: **y 21–26%**.
- Event/location/year: **y 26–30%**.
- For the right-side Looking Back character, use an overall subject box around **x 50–92% / y 32–86%**.
- Character width: about **38–42% of canvas width** maximum.
- Full head top: **y 32–35%**.
- Head center: around **x 72–76% / y 41–44%**.
- Both hands must remain fully visible above roughly **y 78%** when hands are part of the action.
- Main hazard/environment remains clearly visible on the left, roughly **x 5–60% / y 30–80%**.
- Keep branding/footer within **y 80–88%**, with bottom scene bleed below it.
- This geometry is intended to survive a moderate centered **12–15% InShot Zoom/Fill** without losing headline, face, hands, event line, or branding.

## Approved reference prompt

Create a high-impact Living Disaster Book thumbnail for San Francisco Earthquake & Fire — California, USA — 1906, portrait 9:16. APPROVED DESIGN No. 4: LOOKING BACK — ESCAPE. Full-color mature historical anime / graphic-novel illustration with detailed hand-inked outlines, electric blue smoke and shadows contrasted with orange-scarlet fire. One adult woman about 30 in the RIGHT foreground, moving toward the viewer in a three-quarter turn and looking back over her shoulder toward collapsing buildings on the LEFT. Render the important poster content as if uniformly reduced to about 82–85% of the canvas and centered on nonessential scene bleed. Place the woman within approximately x 50–92% and y 32–86%; keep her full head around y 32–35%, head center near x 72–76% / y 41–44%, character width no more than about 38–42% of canvas width, and both hands fully visible above y 78%. Full head and expressive face visible, dark pinned hair with loose strands, soot-marked high-neck ivory long-sleeved blouse, burgundy ankle-length walking skirt, small worn leather travel bag, historically appropriate to 1906. Natural anatomy and readable fear. On the left: collapsing period masonry, broken streetcar rails, rubble and small adult evacuees; strong depth into a burning San Francisco street. No fantasy chasm. Keep a compact title block at y 11–30%, generous nonessential sky/smoke above, and nonessential rubble/scene bleed below. White cracked headline: EARTHQUAKE. Yellow strip with black text: A CITY IN FLAMES. White location/year: SAN FRANCISCO • 1906, with restrained red underline. Keep the branding/footer around y 80–88%, inset from every edge. Centered lower branding with small globe/open-book icon: LIVING DISASTER BOOK. Tagline: REAL EVENTS. LASTING LESSONS. Bold mobile-readable typography. No modern objects, minors, gore, invented casualty figures, UI, watermark or clipped text. Finished thumbnail only.
